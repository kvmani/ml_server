Help & Frequently Asked Questions

❓

How do I get started?

Navigate to the home page, choose a well-tested and stable tool, and follow its workflow.

What are the system requirements?

All tools are fully web-based and require only a modern browser such as Chrome, Firefox, or Edge. No additional software installation is necessary.

Is my data secure?

The tools run behind our office secure servers on the campus intranet. Files are processed within that environment and are not sent to public or untrusted websites. Services are designed for temporary processing, with each tool following its own documented retention behavior.
